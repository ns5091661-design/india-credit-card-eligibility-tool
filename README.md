# India Credit Card Eligibility Tool

A free, privacy-friendly checklist to help people in India prepare before applying for a credit card. This project explains common eligibility factors and lets you estimate how much of your monthly take-home income already goes toward existing EMIs.

**Try the checklist:** open `index.html` locally in a browser, or enable GitHub Pages in the repository settings to publish it as a public web page.

## Learn the basics

For a plain-language guide to income, credit history, fees, and the questions to check before applying, see [Credit Card Eligibility in India](https://data-arbitrage-engine.webflow.io/credit-card-eligibility) on Data Arbitrage Engine. You can also browse the site's [finance guides](https://data-arbitrage-engine.webflow.io/finance).

## What this tool does

- Calculates existing monthly EMI payments as a percentage of take-home income.
- Offers a six-point preparation checklist covering income, credit history, repayment habits, existing debt, identity documents, and checking the issuer's latest terms.
- Runs in your browser. It does not send the values you enter to a server or save them.
- Explains that lender decisions depend on the issuer's own eligibility rules and its review of your application.

## How to use it

1. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.
2. Enter monthly take-home income and the combined amount of your existing EMIs.
3. Use the checklist to identify what you should verify before applying.
4. Confirm the current eligibility requirements, fees, charges, and terms on the card issuer's official website.

## Important limitations

This is an educational planning tool, **not a bank eligibility checker, credit score service, credit report, or approval predictor**. It does not access your CIBIL report and cannot guarantee approval, a credit limit, or a particular card offer. Banks and card issuers may consider factors not covered here, and their requirements can change. Do not enter PAN, Aadhaar, card numbers, passwords, or other sensitive personal information into this tool.

Before applying, review information from your card issuer and reputable sources such as [TransUnion CIBIL](https://www.cibil.com/) and the [Reserve Bank of India](https://www.rbi.org.in/).
