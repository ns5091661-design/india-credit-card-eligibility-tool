What affects credit-card eligibility?

Issuers may consider age, income, employment, credit history, repayment behaviour, current obligations, location, and their own internal policies. The minimum income and acceptable credit profile vary by card and applicant. A published eligibility condition is not a promise of approval.

Use reliable sources

Start with the card issuer's official product terms. You can also review your credit information through TransUnion CIBIL and read consumer-awareness information from the Reserve Bank of India.
